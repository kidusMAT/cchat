# CCHAT design system

## Genre
Tactile editorial / neo-brutalist social product.

## Theme
Near-white paper, black ink and rules, hot-pink accent `#FF2D55`. Dark mode inverts paper and ink while retaining the pink accent.

## Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk, bold, tight tracking, roman.
- Interface and message text: DM Mono.

## Spacing and motion
8px spacing scale. Hard 4–6px offset shadows. Motion is limited to transform/opacity, with reduced-motion support.

## Shared components
Thread card, reaction button, participant chip, live indicator, conversation message, related thread card.

## Page families
- Marketing / landing: marquee hero + live index + public feed.
- App / thread: three-column conversation workbench that collapses to conversation-first mobile.
